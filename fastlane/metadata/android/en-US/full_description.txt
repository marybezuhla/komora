Komora is a simple, open and safe collection of apps. It installs and updates free apps straight from GitHub. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

• Every file is checked before installing: the right app, signed with the pinned key, newer than the installed one.
• New version notices and automatic updates, if you want them; off by default.
• Screenshots (when the app's repository has them), what's new and technical details.
• Other sources: optionally any public GitHub repository, with a warning and a key remembered at the first install.
• Ukrainian and English, light and dark themes with Ukrainian names, or the phone's own colours.

Requires Android 12 or newer.
